What is a Masterclass?

  • A 3.5 hour class, led by experienced and passionate 90+ ATAR tutors.
  • We go through the Unit 3 or Unit 4 content you need to know for your exam, and show you exactly how to achieve full marks.
  • The session is a mix of teaching and practice questions, with tutors guiding you to make sure you're on the right track.
  • You get access to the Young Einsteins revision workbook (and marking key) to help you prepare for the exam.
  • We answer any questions you have, and give tips to help you feel more confident heading into the exam.
